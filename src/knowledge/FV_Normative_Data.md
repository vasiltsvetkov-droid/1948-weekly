## Key Variable Definitions

| Variable | Definition | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| F0 | Theoretical maximal force at zero velocity | N/kg |
| V0 | Theoretical maximal velocity at zero force | m/s |
| Pmax | Peak mechanical power = F0 × V0 / 4 | W/kg |
| RFmax | Ratio of horizontal to total GRF at first step | % |
| DRF | Rate of decline in RF per unit velocity increase | % per m/s |
| FV-Imbalance | Deviation from athlete's optimal F-V profile slope | % |
