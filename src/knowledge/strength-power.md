## Key Postulates

1. **Relative strength is the foundation of football performance.** Back squat 1RM/BW ratios correlate with sprint acceleration, change of direction, and jump performance. A minimum of 1.5x BW squat is the "good" threshold; elite players exceed 2.0x BW (Comfort et al. 2014; Loturco et al. 2018).

2. **In-season strength requires a minimum of 2 sessions per week to prevent detraining.** Frequency of 2 sessions/week at >= 70-80% 1RM with 2-3 sets per exercise maintains strength and power throughout the competitive season (Ronnestad et al. 2011; Raastad et al. 2010). Below this threshold, detraining occurs within 2-4 weeks.

3. **Nordic Hamstring Curls reduce hamstring injury rate by 51%.** The NHC program (Petersen et al. 2011) is the single most evidence-based injury prevention exercise in football. NHC increases biceps femoris fascicle length by 12-24% in 6-10 weeks, and each 0.5 cm gain in fascicle length is associated with ~75% reduction in injury risk (Bourne et al. 2017; Timmins et al. 2016).

4. **Copenhagen Adductor exercises reduce adductor injury by 41%.** The Copenhagen protocol (Haroy et al. 2019) provides eccentric adductor strengthening through side-lying hip adduction. Recommended dose: 2x/week pre-season, 1x/week in-season.

5. **Sprint performance is determined by the Force-Velocity (F-V) profile.** The Morin-Samozino framework (2016) describes the trade-off between maximal force (F0) and maximal velocity (V0). Most football sprints are <20 m (acceleration-dominant), making horizontal force production (F0, RF) the priority for most positions.

6. **Fascicle length gains reverse within 2 weeks of detraining.** Year-round NHC maintenance is required to preserve the protective muscle architecture adaptations. Short BFlh fascicle length (<10.6 cm) confers 4x greater hamstring injury risk (Timmins et al. 2016).

7. **Speed has the shortest residual training effect (5 +/- 3 days).** Maximal speed work must appear in every microcycle to be maintained. Power/speed-strength has a 15 +/- 5 day residual, requiring 2x/week minimum (Issurin 2008).

## Thresholds & Decision Rules

### Strength Benchmarks (1RM / Body Weight)

| Exercise | Good | Excellent | Elite |
|----------|------|-----------|-------|
| Back Squat | 1.5x BW | 1.75x BW | >2.0x BW |
| Romanian Deadlift | 1.4x BW | 1.6x BW | >1.8x BW |
| Hip Thrust | 1.8x BW | 2.0x BW | >2.2x BW |
| Single-Leg Squat (3RM) | 0.6x BW | 0.75x BW | >0.85x BW |
| Nordic Hamstring Curl | 8 reps | 12 reps | >15 reps |
| Bench Press | 0.9x BW | 1.1x BW | >1.3x BW |
| Pull-ups | 8 reps | 12 reps | >15 reps |

### CMJ and Power Benchmarks

| Test | Moderate | Good | Elite |
|------|---------|------|-------|
| CMJ Height | 35-38 cm | 38-45 cm | >45 cm |
| SJ Height | 32-36 cm | 36-42 cm | >42 cm |
| RSI (Drop Jump 40 cm) | 1.0-1.3 | 1.3-1.7 | >1.7 |
| RSI Modified (FT:CT) | 0.45-0.55 | 0.55-0.65 | >0.65 |

### Force-Velocity Profile Benchmarks

| Parameter | Below Average | Average | Above Average | Elite |
|-----------|--------------|---------|---------------|-------|
| F0 (N/kg) | <7.5 | 7.5-8.5 | 8.5-9.5 | >9.5 |
| V0 (m/s) | <9.0 | 9.0-10.5 | 10.5-11.5 | >11.5 |
| Pmax (W/kg) | <18 | 18-22 | 22-26 | >26 |
| RFmax (%) | <50% | 50-55% | 55-60% | >60% |

### F-V Profile Classification

| Profile Type | FVimb | Training Priority |
|-------------|-------|------------------|
| Balanced | <15% | Maintain; general speed work |
| Force-deficit | >15% force-oriented | Heavy resisted sprints; strength (sled 10-20% BW) |
| Velocity-deficit | >15% velocity-oriented | Max speed runs; light resisted sprints |

### Injury Prevention Exercise Evidence

| Exercise | Injury Reduction | Evidence |
|----------|-----------------|----------|
| Nordic Hamstring Curl | 51% hamstring injury reduction | Petersen et al. 2011; van Dyk et al. 2019 |
| Copenhagen Adductor | 41% adductor injury reduction | Haroy et al. 2019 |
| FIFA 11+ warm-up | 37% total injury reduction | Soligard et al. 2008 |
| NHC fascicle length effect | 12-24% BFlh fascicle length increase | Bourne et al. 2017 |

### In-Season Minimum Effective Dose

| Parameter | Requirement |
|-----------|------------|
| Frequency | 2 sessions/week minimum |
| Intensity | >= 70-80% 1RM (maximal strength); >= 60% 1RM (hypertrophy maintenance) |
| Sets | 2-3 sets per exercise |
| Total weekly volume | 4-6 working sets per muscle group (minimum to prevent detraining) |
| Timing | MD-4 (heavy/neural) and MD-3 or MD-2 (power/reactive) |
| Exercises | Compound movements: squat variations, hip thrust, Nordic curl, bench/pull |

### Residual Training Effects

| Quality | Residual Duration | Minimum Maintenance Frequency |
|---------|------------------|-------------------------------|
| Speed (max velocity) | 5 +/- 3 days | Every microcycle (3-4 sessions/week) |
| Power / Speed-strength | 15 +/- 5 days | 2x/week minimum |
| Aerobic endurance | 25-35 days | 1-2x/week |
| Maximal strength | 30 +/- 5 days | 1x/week can maintain |

## Analysis Templates

### S&C Context

```
STRENGTH & CONDITIONING REFERENCE FRAMEWORK:
Strength and power data are not directly captured in the weekly GPS monitoring system.
This knowledge base provides reference benchmarks for clinical and S&C decision-making.

KEY BENCHMARKS:
- Back Squat: good 1.5x BW, excellent 1.75x BW, elite >2.0x BW
- CMJ: moderate 35-38 cm, good 38-45 cm, elite >45 cm
- RSI Modified (FT:CT): moderate 0.45-0.55, good 0.55-0.65, elite >0.65

IN-SEASON PROGRAMMING:
- Minimum 2 strength sessions per week to prevent detraining
- Session A (MD-4): Heavy/neural — squat 3-4x3-5 at 80-90% 1RM, hip thrust, Nordic curl
- Session B (MD-2/3): Power/reactive — hang clean/jump squat 4x3-5 at 60-75%, plyometrics
- NHC maintenance: 1x/week in-season (3x12 reps)
- Copenhagen maintenance: 1x/week in-season

INJURY PREVENTION STACK:
1. FIFA 11+ warm-up (every session, 15-20 min)
2. Nordic Hamstring Curl (51% hamstring injury reduction)
3. Copenhagen Adductor (41% adductor injury reduction)
4. Single-leg stability work (2x/week)
5. Hip strengthening (glute med, hip external rotators)

SPRINT MECHANICS:
- Most football sprints are <20 m (acceleration-dominant)
- Force-deficit players: heavy resisted sprints (sled 10-20% BW), 5-8x20-30 m
- Velocity-deficit players: max speed runs, light resisted sprints
- Speed residual = 5 days; must be trained in every microcycle
```

## Chart Specifications

None. Strength and power data (1RM values, CMJ results, F-V profiles) are not captured in the current GPS monitoring system. These metrics require dedicated testing protocols (force plates, timing gates, isokinetic dynamometers) that exist outside the weekly data pipeline.
